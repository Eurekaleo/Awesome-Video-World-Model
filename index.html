01 The lens

Video is a trace of a latent temporal world

An observed video is partial, lossy, temporally ordered, and bound to a particular projection. The survey separates this visible trace from the entities, relations, dynamics, and state that produced it.

This distinction exposes two different demands. Temporal coherence concerns smooth local transitions in the trace. World consistency concerns persistent entities, relations, and constraints across occlusion, viewpoint change, and long rollouts.
